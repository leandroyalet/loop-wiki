# Pocock — "Tracer Bullets: Keeping AI Slop Under Control" (2026-01-22)

**Author:** [[matt-pocock]] · **Raw:** `raw/2026-10-12-pocock-tracer-bullets.md` · Up: [[sources]]

## Summary
AI's sycophancy leads it to produce *"complete solutions all at once"*, building whole horizontal layers in the dark, or *"outrunning your headlights"*. The cure from *The Pragmatic Programmer* is the **tracer bullet**: a tiny end-to-end slice through all the layers. You build it, test it, get feedback, and then *"move to the next slice in a fresh context window."*

## Key claims
- Slop creates a *"huge review burden"*. See [[operator-debts]].
- You must be *"extremely explicit"* in the loop prompt. A ready-made "Tracer Bullets" prompt section is provided. See [[writing-a-loop-prompt]].
- *"the principles apply harder to AI than they ever did to humans. Context window constraints make the discipline non-negotiable."*
