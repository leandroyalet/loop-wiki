# Boris Cherny

Head of [[claude-code]] at [[anthropic]]. His remark helped spark the term: *"I don't prompt Claude anymore. I have loops running that prompt Claude and figuring out what to do. My job is to write loops"* ([[src-osmani-loop-engineering]], [[src-tns-loop-engineering]]). Up: [[entities]].

On verification: giving Claude a verification process is *"probably the biggest key to higher quality outcomes"*, and he estimates it improves output quality **2–3×** (as reported in [[src-osmani-claude-code-code-review]], which calls him the creator of Claude Code). See [[verification]].

Osmani's reading: *"Cherny's point isn't that the work got easier. It's that the leverage point moved."*
