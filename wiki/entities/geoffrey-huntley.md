# Geoffrey Huntley

Creator of the **Ralph** technique ([[ralph-loop]]), first written up 2025-07-14 ([[src-huntley-ralph]]), about a year before "loop engineering" had a name. He used Ralph to build **CURSED**, an esoteric programming language that, as he notes, was not in the LLM's training data. Up: [[entities]].

- Contributed: [[backpressure]], one item per loop, *"deterministically allocate the stack"*, tuning with signs ([[writing-a-loop-prompt]]), and the main-context-as-scheduler idea ([[orchestrator-workers]]).
- [[matt-pocock]] credits him as the originator and turned the technique into a step-by-step recipe ([[src-pocock-getting-started-with-ralph]]). See [[building-a-ralph-loop]].
- Positions: loops are best for greenfield projects. He questions human-centred maintainability, yet insists *"engineers are still needed."* See [[operator-debts]].
