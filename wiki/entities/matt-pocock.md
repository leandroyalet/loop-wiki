# Matt Pocock

TypeScript educator who runs **AI Hero** (aihero.dev). He is the most prolific practitioner source in the wiki: 12 sources ingested, from January to September 2026. He popularised [[geoffrey-huntley]]'s [[ralph-loop]] with concrete scripts, and he maintains a widely used skills library (`mattpocock/skills`). Up: [[entities]].

## Contributions
- **Ralph made practical:** HITL `ralph-once.sh` → capped AFK `afk-ralph.sh`, Docker sandboxes, PRD + `progress.txt`, and the `<promise>COMPLETE</promise>` sigil ([[src-pocock-getting-started-with-ralph]], [[src-pocock-ralph-tips]]). See [[building-a-ralph-loop]].
- **Fresh context per iteration:** a critique of in-session loops, built on the [[smart-zone]] idea ([[src-pocock-ralph-plugin]]).
- **Vocabulary:** AFK vs HITL, software factory, dark factory, automated check vs automated review, handoff artifact, AX ([[src-pocock-ai-coding-dictionary]]). See [[afk]], [[software-factory]] and [[agent-experience]].
- **Discipline via classics:** tracer bullets, TDD in vertical slices, deep modules ([[src-pocock-tracer-bullets]], [[src-pocock-tdd-skill]], [[src-pocock-codebases-agents-love]]). See [[task-sizing]].
- **Workflow around the loop:** the seven phases ([[src-pocock-seven-phases]]) and the triage state machine ([[src-pocock-triage-skill]]).

## Positions
- He is pro-loop but HITL-first: most of his own usage is HITL, with AFK reserved for low-risk work ([[src-pocock-ralph-tips]]).
- *"Ralph works because it's ruthlessly simple. Keep it that way"* ([[src-pocock-ralph-plugin]]).
